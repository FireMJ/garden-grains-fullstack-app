# Garden & Grains style guide

The site intentionally follows the visual language of the Nkora reference while using Garden & Grains content and media.

## Foundation

- Display: the local Garden & Grains display face copied from `nkora-v4-regular.woff2`, regular weight only.
- Body: Inter, regular and medium weights.
- Espresso `#24201a`, cream `#fef5b3`, olive `#6f7531`, rust `#a25028`.
- Order dialog surface: warm neutral white `#f5f4ef`; cream remains reserved for the primary brand surfaces and accents.
- Display copy is uppercase, tightly led, and large enough to become part of the composition.
- Body copy stays narrow, calm, and readable; use cream or espresso at reduced opacity for secondary text.

## Components

- Header: fixed, centered text wordmark with an ampersand at 60% scale, cream navigation, dark translucent surface after scrolling.
- Buttons: compact pill shapes; cream primary, cream outline, or espresso on light backgrounds.
- Images: full-bleed editorial crops, mostly square, 4:5, or full-height; use subtle corner rounding only where sections meet.
- Menu: large category headings, quiet dividers, text-first item rows, and editorial photography.
- Dialog: cream surface, espresso controls, 900px maximum width, near-full-screen mobile height, sticky header/footer.
- Footer: cream outer field with a compact olive rounded panel and all primary links on one bottom line.

## Motion and accessibility

- Reveal content with a short upward fade; do not animate every element independently.
- Keep hover rotation and scale changes subtle.
- Honour reduced-motion preferences.
- Every interactive element needs a visible keyboard focus state, meaningful label, and sufficient touch target.
